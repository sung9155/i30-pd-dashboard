i30n.bin, lamp_base.png and lamp_glow.png in this folder are an adaptation of a 3D model:

This work is based on "hyundai i30 n" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hyundai-i30-n-90c3bd24390b46299cf01c16d5e8091f) by amogusstrikesback2 (https://sketchfab.com/amogusstrikesback2) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes: converted by scripts/car3d_model.py: light-beam cones, lens-flare sprites and the tail lamp picture decal
removed, number plates made plain parts the app draws (front plate edges painted grille black), body / tyre / rim
textures baked into vertex colours, body paint marked so the app can recolour it, wheels and callipers split for
steering, lamp parts sorted into lamp groups (tail lamp lens cut finer), turn-signal, tail and high-mount stop lamp
glow recoloured. Only the lamp / chrome texture atlas is kept as images (lamp_base.png, lamp_glow.png).
